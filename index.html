About $ROOTED

What $ROOTED Is

$ROOTED is an ERC-20 token on Base, paired with Seeds — a collectible NFT (ERC-721) you mint, grow, and eventually harvest for $ROOTED rewards. Total Seed supply is fixed at 80,000; the token itself has no admin mint function, so no one can create more $ROOTED out of thin air after launch. Every $ROOTED transfer carries a small tax split between a permanent burn (which shrinks supply) and a treasury, which funds the buyback mechanism below.

Buying and Selling

This site has two different things you can trade, and they work in two different places. Seeds (the NFTs) are bought and sold right here, in the Marketplace section above. $ROOTED (the token) is not sold here — it trades on Uniswap, a separate app where people swap one crypto token for another. To buy or sell $ROOTED, go to Uniswap and use the $ROOTED contract address: 0x87605cccb17bc26111059eC9c4d5EC88Fc0B5da6 on Base.

How Seeds Work

Minting a Seed costs ETH and gives you an NFT with a randomly assigned tier — Common, Uncommon, Notable, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, or Genesis — decided by Chainlink VRF, a verifiable randomness service that proves on-chain the result wasn't picked or influenced by us. Rarer tiers unlock more $ROOTED yield. Your Seed shows as "Revealing..." until VRF responds — this is usually quick, but VRF runs on its own independent network and its response time isn't something this site (or we) can control, so it can occasionally take longer. Nothing is lost while you wait; just check back and refresh "My Collection" later. Once a Seed matures to Tree stage or beyond, you choose a path: Orchard (steady $ROOTED yield over time), GenesisPath (harvest — spend a mature Seed's growth to mint a brand-new Seed), or Hybrid (both, for high-enough tiers). Common-tier Seeds can also periodically roll a chance to mature further. Each wallet can only mint up to a capped number of Seeds (see "Minted by you" above once connected) rather than an unlimited amount — that's deliberate, so early distribution spreads across more real holders instead of one wallet or bot cornering a large share of the collection.

The Banker

RootedBuyback ("the banker") is a separate contract that buys $ROOTED from the open market using ETH from the treasury tax, then permanently burns whatever it buys. It's permissionless — anyone, not just us, can trigger a buyback — and it enforces its own limits on how much it spends per call and how often, checking the market price against a trusted reference before acting so it can't be tricked into overpaying during a single manipulated trade. Every buyback is logged on-chain as a public event.

How We're Trying to Prevent a Rug Pull

Once setup and testing are complete, ownership of every contract in this project — the token, the Seed NFT, the marketplace, and the banker — will be permanently renounced. After that, no one, including us, will hold any admin key, backdoor, or ability to mint extra tokens, change fees, pause trading, or withdraw funds. All contract source code is verified and public (links below), total supply is fixed, and the banker's buy-and-burn logic runs with no human in the loop. None of this makes $ROOTED risk-free — it's a new, small project, and you should only ever put in money you could fully afford to lose. This isn't financial advice; do your own research.
